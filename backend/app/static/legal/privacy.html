Privacy Policy

Last updated {{updated}}

The Travel Edit turns travel posts you share into saved places and trip plans. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other companies' apps and websites.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and first name, or the private identifier Apple gives us if you sign in with Apple. Passwords are stored only as a secure hash.
  • What you save: links to posts you share, screenshots you add, and the places, lists, trips, notes, bookings and packing lists you create.
  • Location: only when you allow it, and only while you use a feature that needs it (sorting places by distance, the map, today's weather). Your precise location is used for that request and isn't stored with your account.
  • Purchases: if you subscribe to Plus, Apple sends us the subscription's status and dates. We never see your payment details.
  • Notifications: a device token, if you turn on notifications, so we can tell you when a save is ready.
  • Diagnostics: crash and error reports with your account ID (never your email or saved places), so we can fix problems.

How we use it

Only to run the app: finding the places mentioned in a post, showing them on maps and in your trips, syncing across your devices, sending the notifications you asked for, providing Plus, and fixing bugs. We don't use your content for advertising.

Services that process data for us

  • Anthropic (Claude) reads a shared post's caption, transcript and still frames to find the places in it, and helps plan trips. Anthropic doesn't use this data to train its models.
  • Google Maps Platform looks up place details and photos.
  • Fly.io hosts our servers and stores your data.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, notifications and in-app purchases.
  • Sentry receives crash and error reports.
  • Open-Meteo provides weather for a trip's city or your current area.

To read a post you share, our servers fetch it from the service it's on (for example TikTok, Instagram or YouTube). The video itself isn't kept once the places are found.

Sharing

Trips you invite people to are visible to the people on them, and a list or trip you share by link is visible to anyone with the link until you stop sharing it. Otherwise we only share data with the services above, or when required by law.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile > Delete account); this permanently removes your account and everything you've saved. A subscription is managed by Apple, so cancel it in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions as well.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it, by contacting us below. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have further rights, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The Travel Edit isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
