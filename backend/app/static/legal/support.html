Saving a post

In TikTok, Instagram or YouTube, tap Share, then The Travel Edit (if you don't see it, scroll the row of apps to the end, tap More, and turn it on). The places in the post appear in your library within a minute. If a post can't be read, save a screenshot of it instead: tap + in the app and choose Screenshots.

Plus

Plus gives unlimited video saves. To cancel, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. If you reinstalled the app or changed phones, open Profile > Plan in the app and tap Restore Purchases. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Profile and tap Delete account. Everything you've saved is removed permanently.
